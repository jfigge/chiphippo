Implement wider-chip seating for 600-mil DIP packages in ChipHippo.

Background: every DIP chip currently seats and draws at a fixed three-pitch straddle, rows e and f, regardless of its real package width. The 600-mil parts, meaning the 6502, wide memory chips, and DIP-24, 28, 32 and 40, are actually 0.6 inches wide and should seat six pitches apart, at rows d and h. The 300-mil parts are correct as they are and must keep seating at e and f.

Required changes, in four parts. First, seating logic: update pinOffset in footprints.js and chipSeat in seating.js so a 600-mil package's anchor and derived pin rows resolve to d and h instead of e and f, driven by the existing body field in the package table. Second, rendering: update chipBox and chipBodyBox in chip-view.js so a 600-mil chip draws at its true six-pitch height, with the body slab covering the extra rows it now spans. Third, hole blocking: the holes now covered by the wider body, meaning rows e, f and g under the chip, must be blocked from wiring. This reaches occupancy.js, the wire tool, hit-testing, and the auto-router's free-hole counts, so none of them let a wire land in a hole the chip's body now covers. Fourth, tests: update the existing tests that assume every DIP straddles e and f so they reflect six-pitch seating for 600-mil packages.

Explicitly out of scope: no migration for existing saved desks. A 600-mil chip already seated at e and f on a previously saved desk must be left exactly as it is; the new six-pitch seating only applies to chips placed or moved from now on. Do not touch the 300-mil seating path at all.
